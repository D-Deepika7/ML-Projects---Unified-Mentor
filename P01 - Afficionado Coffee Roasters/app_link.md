
# Afficionado Coffee Roasters — Live Dashboard

**Live App:** [Open the Streamlit Dashboard](https://ml-projects-um-deepika-p01.streamlit.app/)

## Project Description

An interactive Streamlit dashboard developed for **Product Optimization & Revenue Contribution Analysis** at Afficionado Coffee Roasters.

The application provides insights into product performance, sales volume, revenue contribution, and store-level performance using transaction data.

### Key Features

- **Dashboard Overview:** Key performance indicators, including total revenue, units sold, transaction records, and product coverage.
- **Category Revenue Contribution:** Visualizes revenue distribution across product categories.
- **Product Rankings:** Identifies leading products by revenue and sales volume.
- **Store Performance:** Compares revenue and sales contribution across store locations.
- **Popularity vs Revenue Analysis:** Examines the relationship between product sales volume and revenue contribution.
- **Product Drill-Down:** Enables detailed exploration of individual product performance.
- **Interactive Filters:** Allows users to filter results by store location, product category, and product type.

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Plotly

## Objective

To transform retail transaction data into actionable business insights that support product performance evaluation, revenue contribution analysis, and data-informed product optimization decisions.

**Note:** Revenue represents sales revenue, not profit, as product cost and margin data are not included in the dataset.
