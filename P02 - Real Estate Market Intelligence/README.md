# Title: Machine Learning-Based Buyer Segmentation and Investment Profiling for Real Estate Market Intelligence

The project aims to use unsupervised machine learning, primarily K-Means clustering, to identify distinct groups of real-estate buyers based on their demographic, geographic, financing, purchasing-purpose, and satisfaction characteristics.

The workflow is:
Customer data → Data cleaning → EDA → Feature engineering → Encoding & scaling → Clustering → Cluster evaluation → Buyer profiling → Business recommendations

The resulting buyer segments will be analyzed to understand:
•	Who the different types of buyers are
•	Their investment vs. personal-use preferences
•	Their financing/loan behavior
•	Geographic patterns
•	Demographic characteristics
•	Satisfaction levels

Finally, the findings will be presented through a Streamlit interactive dashboard, allowing users to filter and explore buyer segments by country, region, acquisition purpose, and client type.
The ultimate goal is to help a real-estate company make better marketing, customer targeting, and investment-related decisions using data rather than treating all buyers identically.
